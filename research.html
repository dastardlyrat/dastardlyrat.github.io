Mining and research boundaries

Indexing public work for discovery is distinct from automated extraction of individual creators' identities, habitual methods or personal fingerprints. Access is not consent. Evaluate source permissions, citation, provenance, and any explicit restrictions before reuse.

Material in the two owners' similarly named theory repositories is not assumed to be synchronized. Published descriptions of a seed, gate or simulated component do not imply implementation or autonomous authority.

Source-first public navigation; status, license and evidence are document-specific. The website is not a runtime authority.
